In the tultumous storms of the Florence Seas, the waves crashed on to the ship. Alphonse looked into the grey skies, where it looked as 
though the crow's nest of the ship struck a hole into the grand canvas of thunder and lightning. His gray hair flew against the winds.
His fingers which carried silver and platinum rings ran through his hairs, while his exhale fumed off white fog. 

"How's it going... mister captain?" the man sneered from his behind. Alphonse turned around, his cape flying in the air, so was his
navy apparel, with the golden lines. His skin was pure as a the elves in the valleys of life, and his eyes as sharp 
as the dragon's flame. 

"We're almost there, Alrad. The woman from the Valrine village, considering she was indeed right." the captain smiled, his gray eyes
shining, and casting a great reflection of Alrad in them. 

"Were you in love with her, Alph?" Alrad asked. 

The intensity of the smile on Alph's face lightened, as he replied, "Well... that's a question."

"Were you or not?" Alrad's voice sounded sharper, "Did you cry when the girl was being chewed by the bear, Alphonse!!" he yelled. 

Alphonse looked away from Alrad as he replied, "The Valley of Life answers all."

An old crooked voice rung from beside Alph, "Looks like you two are getting along." The old man's steps thudded 
across the wooden mast, and when Alph turned around, he saw the cloaked man with white hair drooped over
his shoulders and bald on his head. "Is Alrad troubling you, Alph?" the man asked. 

"Nothing to be not ignored, Karsiln." Alphonse sighed, taking a deep breath. 

However, a wave of sadness still loomed deep inside Alphonse's heart. 

'All these people around me... they think they can hurt me. Hehh!' Alphonse scoffed. 'What do they know about me? How'd my life go? 
When I was born, I was held there. Naked. My body dripping of blood of my mother... There was the darkness that enveloped me through echoes 
of the people that were laughing at me.' he coughed, 'And yet I couldn't speak. All I could do was cry. While every single person around me 
laughed and smiled, I was there unable to walk or crawl, even hold my head straight... I got his old, purging through the evil echoes
of laughter that still looms in my head. Compared to those demons, these are nothing... nothing at all.'
